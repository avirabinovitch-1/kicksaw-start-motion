# Kickoff: what this stage is for

Introduce the delivery team and start the project on a positive note. Get on the same page about the project, set expectations for discovery, and leave with owners named and the first discovery session booked. The deck is built from the SOW, the handoff packet when there is one, and the sales calls; every slide is a copy of a finished template on the Kicksaw design system, and only its content changes. The client leaves confident in the team and ready to go.

## Calls the PM and SC make together

- Is there a timeline worth showing? Only when phases and go-live are firm; a tight SOW window is labelled a draft
- Which weeks are breaks? Holiday weeks lose most of their meeting time
- Who presents each slide? The owner role is the default; the person's name goes in the speaker notes
- Which optional slides fit this client?
- What does the client need to bring to week 1? Access, data samples, attendees; that is the next steps table

## The bar: a kickoff deck is ready when

- It reads as a warm first introduction, not a status meeting
- Why we're here and success metrics are in the client's own terms
- What we're building matches the SOW, this phase and future phase
- Every person has a photo, role, what they lead and how the client works with them
- Client roles are named: business sponsor, project champion, product owner, SMEs
- Discovery sessions each have a focus, agenda and attendees
- Communication, tools, risks and how the client can help are covered
- Next steps are a table of actions with owners
- Presenters are in speaker notes, and every assumption is noted on its slide
- The client knows what the first discovery session covers and who should attend
