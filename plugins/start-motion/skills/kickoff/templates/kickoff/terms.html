Salesforce in plain language

The words you will hear in every session, in Tidewell terms

Lead

Someone who asked for a quote and has not met a closer yet

Account

The home or business we are selling to

Contact

A person at that home or business

Opportunity

A deal a closer is working, from consult to signed

Quote

The priced proposal, with panels and adders

Case

An install issue someone needs to fix

Campaign

A marketing effort, like a home show, and the leads it brought

Dashboard

Live charts of the pipeline, by office and closer
