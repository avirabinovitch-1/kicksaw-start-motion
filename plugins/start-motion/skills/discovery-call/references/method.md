# Discovery call: what this stage is for

Discovery learns how the client works today and captures it as workflows and requirements the client has confirmed. Everything in the solution readout comes from here. The call processor turns every call, the same day, into the discovery documents: the requirements list, the workflow document, the What we heard page and the discovery plan, with a draft user story for every requirement. Nothing reaches the readout that the client has not seen in a weekly playback.

## When you review the documents after a call

- Each new requirement reads in plain language and says one thing
- The workflow steps match how the client described the work, not how it is designed on paper
- Every open question has a person's name on it
- Anything past the SOW is marked Past SOW and the PM hears about it this week, not at the readout
- Only the client's words become requirements; a Kicksaw suggestion is an open item marked as ours

## The bar: discovery is ready to close when

- Every session's must-leave-with items are done, or moved with a reason; every SOW line on the plan has been covered
- Every in-scope SOW item maps to at least one epic
- Every requirement has an ID, its source call, speaker and priority
- Every core workflow is mapped end to end, each step tracing to its requirements
- Every workflow has been played back to the client lead
- Every open question has a named owner and a date; anything that blocks design is flagged
- Anything heard past the SOW is on the scope watch list and the PM knows
- The requirements list, its draft user stories and the workflows are current through the last call

## Calls the skill cannot make for you

- Who is in the room: the person who does the work, not only their manager
- Which processes go first: the ones with the most volume or the most pain
- Which workflows need a full diagram and which a short note
- What stays internal: staffing, commercial and SOW risk
