# Readout: what this stage is for

Show the client we understood them, what we recommend building, and what is in scope. They review it now and sign the updated version at scope sign-off. Everything comes from the three discovery documents; nothing is written from a transcript. The readout is the written reference the client digests after the call; the deck is cut from it, never built separately, and carries the same figures.

## How every readout flows

Give me the high level, tell me what you heard, tell me what you will build, then how we get there.

- Executive summary: what are we here to do, and what do you need from me? Three pillars, the three approvals with a date, Figure 1
- What we heard: did you understand us? On by default; cut for clients who already know their process
- What we will build: what are you going to do about it? The build tied to what it solves, and every workflow the client played back
- How we get there: what is in, what is not, what happens next

## Calls the SC makes on every readout

- What we heard: in or out? In by default; cut it when the client documented their own process and would resent the playback
- Timeline or no timeline? Dates only when you are confident in them; otherwise name the dependency that sets the pace
- Workflows in the deck or the appendix? Played back in discovery means appendix; the deck keeps the overview and at most two journeys
- Requirement IDs in the readout? Only when the client asked for the full list
- Who has to be in the room? The business sponsor, who signs the scope. If they are out, move the readout
- Something past the SOW came up? A roadmap item with a size and a trade-off; never widen scope on the call

## The bar: ready to present when

- Every pain point has a response, or the what-we-heard group was cut on purpose
- Every confirmed requirement appears in a workflow or in scope-out
- Anything past the SOW is named as a future phase or a change order
- The three approvals and the sign-off date are at the top
- The SA has reviewed the build, the system at a glance and the sequence
- Every figure in the deck is identical to its figure in the readout
- Every slide has a header; nothing reads bottom-up
- The dry run is done and its cuts are in
- Readout, deck and the requirements list sit on one share link in the project repository
- The business sponsor, who signs the scope, is on the invite
