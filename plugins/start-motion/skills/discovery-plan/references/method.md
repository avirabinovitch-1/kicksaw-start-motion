# Discovery plan: what this stage is for

Decide which sessions discovery needs, who has to be in each, and what each one has to leave with. Not from a generic list: from the SOW, which says what has to be true by sign-off, and the sales calls, which usually answer a third to half of what a generic plan would ask. The sessions come from what is left between the two. The plan is internal; the client sees only the session list and the inputs request.

## Planning the sessions from what you already know

- Sort every SOW line: known (the client said it, with a quote), needs confirming (heard once, or sales said it for them), or unknown. The unknowns are the questions; the questions are the sessions
- Open with what you heard: a known item is a two-minute confirmation at the top of the session, never a re-ask
- Name who, and why: every session has someone who decides, someone who knows, and someone who does the work. Never only the client lead
- Must leave with: the two or three things a session cannot end without. That is what tells you what to cut mid-call
- Time the agenda: 60 or 90 minutes, items that add up, the long slots on the unknowns. Two sessions a day at most; check the holidays in the window
- Check coverage: no SOW line without a session, no session without a SOW reason. Discovery is the only window where scope and cost can still change

## Calls the SC makes

- What do we already know? Read the SOW and the sales calls before planning anything
- Which processes go first? The ones with the most volume or the most pain; the reverse demo usually makes this obvious
- Who is in the room? The person who does the work, not only their manager
- What must this session leave with? Decide before the call, not during it
- Is this past the SOW? Log it on the scope watch list with the quote and tell the PM that week
- What stays internal? Staffing, commercial and SOW risk stay on the plan and off anything client-facing

## The bar: the plan is ready when

- Every SOW line is on the plan, marked known, needs confirming or unknown, with the client's quote where it is known
- Every session has who is in the room and why, what we already heard, a must-leave-with list and a timed agenda
- Every question has an owner and a session; every input a by-when; every decision a target session
- No SOW line is left without a session, and no session is without a SOW reason
- The session list and the inputs request are ready for the kickoff deck and the client, with nothing internal on them
