# SOW and handoff: what this stage is for

Give delivery everything sales learned before we meet the client. The team walks in prepared, and the client never has to repeat themselves. Sales builds and shares the packet (every sales call transcript, the signed SOW and contracts, the handoff document on DJ's standard); delivery reads it, sends its questions ahead of the call, and accepts the packet on a recorded handoff call. Every gap goes back to sales with an owner and a date.

## What makes the handoff work

- 24 hours is the floor: if the packet is late, the call moves. The reading time is the point
- Questions reach sales before the call, so sales brings answers, not guesses
- Every gap gets an owner and a date, never "we'll find out in discovery"
- The client never repeats themselves: anything already in a transcript is not asked again at kickoff or in discovery
- Sales introduces the team with a warm intro email to the sponsor, and attends kickoff
- Sales stays reachable, named for questions through the first weeks of discovery

## The bar: a handoff is ready when

- The packet went out at least 24 hours before the handoff call
- The folder holds every sales call transcript, named by date and call
- The signed SOW and every contractual document are in the folder and confirmed final
- The handoff doc follows DJ's standard; a section with nothing to say reads "Not discussed in sales"
- Anything promised on a call that is not in the SOW is flagged, with the quote
- The SOW agrees with what was said internally (billing, built versus surfaced, go-live), and every promise that needs a license is covered by the order form; any gap is flagged
- Delivery's questions went to sales before the call
- Every question is answered on the call, or owned with a date
- The answers are folded back into the handoff doc, and the call is recorded

## Questions only this client can answer

Prompts for delivery's questions, not a script. Why they bought (what made them sign now, who else they looked at, what would make them call this a failure). People (who really decides, who was skeptical, who we work with day to day). What was promised (anything said on a call that is not in the SOW, dates promised, what we said we would not do). How it was sized (where the hours came from, which assumptions sit behind them, which part is thinnest). The environment (systems, integrations and data; whether they run Salesforce today; PHI and the BAA). Watch-outs (a prior partner, sensitivity on budget and hours).
