TS-27 · LEAD MANAGEMENT

Duplicate lead warning before saving

As a sales rep, I need to be warned when the lead I am saving already exists, so that two reps never call the same homeowner.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

1

When a lead is saved with an email or phone that matches an open lead or a contact, the user sees a warning listing the matches before the save completes.

2

The user can open a match from the warning, or save the new lead anyway.

3

A lead saved after a warning records that a duplicate warning was shown.

4

Leads created by the web form are saved without a warning and flagged as a possible duplicate.

OPEN QUESTIONS

QUESTION 4 · CRITERION 1

What counts as a duplicate?

Our proposal: the same email, or the same phone number.

QUESTION 5 · CRITERION 1

Does a matching street address count?

Our proposal: no. Two homeowners at one address is common.

QUESTION 6 · CRITERION 4

What happens to a web form that matches?

Our proposal: it saves, and is flagged for a rep to review.
