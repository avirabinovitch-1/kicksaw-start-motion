TS-24 · LEAD MANAGEMENT

First call due within one business day of a new lead

As a sales manager, I need every new lead to show when its first call is due, so that I can see which leads are at risk of waiting more than one business day.

QUESTION 3 · CRITERION 2

Which hours and holidays count toward the business day?

WHAT WE KNOW

The sales team works 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. Pacific, Monday to Friday. We have not heard which holidays the office closes for.

OUR PROPOSAL

Those hours, skipping the company holidays on the HR calendar. A lead that arrives after hours starts its clock the next morning.
