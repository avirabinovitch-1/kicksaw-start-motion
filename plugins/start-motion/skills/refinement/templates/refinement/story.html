TS-21 · LEAD MANAGEMENT

Partner leads routed to the partner manager's queue

As a partner manager, I need leads that come from a referral partner assigned to my queue, so that a partner's referral is never worked by a sales rep who does not know the partner.

QUESTION 1 · CRITERION 1

Which lead sources count as a partner lead?

WHAT WE KNOW

Leads carry nine sources today. On October 7 we heard that roofers and builders both send referrals, and that purchased lists are sometimes logged as partner leads.

OUR PROPOSAL

Roofer referral and Builder referral count as partner leads. Purchased lists do not.

QUESTION 2 · CRITERION 2

How should the partner manager be told about a new lead?

WHAT WE KNOW

Today the partner manager checks a shared inbox each morning and sometimes misses a referral for a day.

OUR PROPOSAL

A Salesforce notification when the lead arrives, and one email each morning listing the new partner leads.
