# Refinement: what this stage is for

Ahead of each sprint, walk the client's product owner through the stories that sprint will build: the story, the questions still open on it, and its acceptance criteria. The session ends with those questions answered and the stories approved, so nothing goes into a sprint that the client has not read and agreed to. The refinement deck is the client-friendly way to do that: it is sent ahead so the product owner arrives prepared, and it is on the screen during the call. A consultant who runs the call from Jira still sends it ahead.

## Calls the SC makes for each session

- Which stories? The ones the next sprint needs, in build order, then the ones the sprint after it depends on. A story from the last session that still has a question open comes back first
- How many? Six to ten on a typical call. Complex stories, or stories with several questions each, bring it closer to three or four
- Is the proposal right? Every question goes to the client with Kicksaw's proposal. The SC reads each one before the deck is built and changes any they would not stand behind
- Does a question need its own slide? Only when a picture explains it better than a sentence: a rule over time, things grouped, who can do what
- Who needs to be on the call? The product owner always. The SA when a story carries a design question

## The bar: a refinement session is ready when

- The stories are in build order, and the next sprint's stories come first
- The number of stories fits the call, with time for the questions on each
- Every story and criterion on a slide reads as it does in Jira
- Every question says what we know, gives our proposal, and names the criterion it affects
- A proposal is something the SC would stand behind, and nothing in it was invented
- A slide can be read on a shared screen; a long story goes over two slides
- Nothing internal is on the slides or in the notes: sizing, hours, build details
- The product owner has the deck ahead of the call
- The SA is on the invite if a story carries a design question
- The call is set to record

## And the session is done when

- Every answer is in the story it changed, with the product owner's words and the time on the recording
- Every question still open has an owner and a date
- The stories the product owner approved have moved on in the Jira workflow
- Anything asked for beyond the signed scope is on the requirements list as Beyond SOW, and the PM knows
