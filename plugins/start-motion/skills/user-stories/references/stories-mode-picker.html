The backlog is yours and your name is on it. Claude is the writer drafting it from discovery. In Work together you read each story as it is finished. In Run with it you get the whole backlog in Jira at once, with the stories that need you pointed out.

Either way the stories are written to the same standard, checked by the same two reviewers, and nothing goes to the client until you move it to Pending Client Approval. You can switch at any point by saying so.
